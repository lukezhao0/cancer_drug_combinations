## Caveats

- **Synergy hits with negative killing** (`syn_hit_low_kill`): 958 (7.1% of synergy hits). Bliss can be large when the pair barely kills or a single drug stimulates growth. Most frequent drugs: MITOTANE (119), RALOXIFENE (75), HYDROXYUREA (74), ESTRAMUSTINE PHOSPHATE (73), ERLOTINIB (70). Kept as hits (design definition); flagged.
- **SR expression is imputed** (`expression_imputed`): CellMiner's SR RNA-seq sample is a mix-up (it matches RPMI-8226). SR's expression is translated from CellMiner's own microarrays (leave-one-out deviation r = 0.94, from the 1f report). SR-specific genes are compressed (ALK). SR is never held out.
- **Borrowed mutation calls**: SNB-19 (from U-251 MG), NCI-H322M (from NCI-H322), HL-60(TB) (from HL-60, also its DepMap proxy). None is held out.
- **Drug targets**: chembl 96, chembl_proxy 2, none_in_chembl 3 (pool drugs). `none_in_chembl` drugs are kept (decision 8).
- **Bliss is noisy**: between the two stored copies of one experiment, Pearson r is 0.209 for Bliss and 0.575 for CSS (1e report).
- **Pairs measured only on held-out lines**: 1 (never selectable).
