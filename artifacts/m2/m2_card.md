## Caveats

- Counts are title/abstract co-mentions, not evidence that the pair was tested together.
- Short abbreviations (e.g. 5-FU, ara-C, ATRA, SAHA, DTIC) are included where widely used; a few may match unrelated text.
- `tumor*` also matches 'tumor necrosis factor', and a few drugs have non-cancer uses (e.g. sirolimus and paclitaxel on heart stents). Checked for that pair: 16 of its 234 records mention stents and 5 match only through 'tumor necrosis'. It is famous either way.
- SN-38 is counted under its own names, not irinotecan's: the pool drug is the metabolite.
- Fame describes the literature, not what a given language model has seen.
